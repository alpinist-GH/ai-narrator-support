## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I contact support?
Email us directly at [alpinist@gmail.com](mailto:alpinist@gmail.com). Please include your app version, device model, and OS version with a brief description of what you are experiencing.

### How does Scan & Listen protect my privacy?
AI Narrator processes Scan & Listen pages entirely on your device. Captured images are stored in a private temporary directory during your session, used solely for local OCR and speech playback, and deleted when the session ends. No photos or document contents are uploaded to external servers.

### What file formats are supported?
- **Presentations**: Microsoft PowerPoint (`.pptx`)
- **Documents & Books**: PDF (`.pdf`), Plain Text (`.txt`)
- **Audio Exports**: Chapterized Audiobooks (`.m4b`), Waveform Audio (`.wav`), MP3 (`.mp3`)

### What should I do if speech generation fails or audio does not play?
1. Ensure your device has enough free storage space for temporary audio caching.
2. Check that your device volume is turned up and Silent / Do Not Disturb mode is not muting audio playback.
3. Restart the app. If the problem persists, email [alpinist@gmail.com](mailto:alpinist@gmail.com).
